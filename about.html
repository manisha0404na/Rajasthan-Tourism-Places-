About Rajasthan

Rajasthan is known as the Land of Kings. It is famous for its forts, palaces, desert and colourful culture.

Rajasthan is the largest state of India. Its capital is Jaipur, which is also called the Pink City.

Culture of Rajasthan

People of Rajasthan wear colourful clothes like Pagri and Ghagra-Choli. Folk dance like Ghoomar and Kalbelia is very famous here. The food like Dal Baati Churma is loved by everyone.

Right To Information

Every citizen has the right to get information from the government under the RTI Act 2005.
